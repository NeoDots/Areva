How To Use

1. Scoop a generous amount of body butter using clean fingers.
2. Warm between your palms to soften the whipped cream.
3. Massage gently onto your arms, legs, elbows, and knees.
4. Apply immediately after your shower to lock in maximum hydration.

Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry place below 30°C (86°F) to prevent melting. Avoid storing in direct sunlight or warm cabinets. Close the jar tightly after use to prevent drying.
